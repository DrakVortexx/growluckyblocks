Grow Lucky Blocks: How to Play

Welcome to Grow Lucky Blocks! Your goal is to grow lucky blocks, hatch creatures, and collect rewards. Here’s everything you need to know to get started.

Basics

Farm plots: Plant seeds in your plots to grow them. Each harvested seed gives you a luck-based lucky block.

Lucky blocks: Place them on your pedestals and open them to hatch creatures.

Creatures: They provide passive income (money/s), and roam/floating animations make your world lively.

Seed Shack: Buy seeds or upgrades to grow blocks faster and unlock rarer blocks.

Premium Currency: Stardust

Stardust is purchased with real money.

Use Stardust to:

Speed up growth

Unlock exclusive lucky blocks

Increase chances for rare creatures and rewards

Bypass wait times on harvesting or pedestal limits

Blue Moon

Occasionally, a Blue Moon occurs, boosting all lucky block rewards and rare creature chances.

Watch the sky! During Blue Moon, your harvests and openings are extra lucky.

Controls

WASD / Arrows → Move your character

Mouse → Look around

E → Interact (plant, harvest, quick buy)

F → Open a lucky block on a filled pedestal

Q → Open Seed Shack UI

TAB → Open/close inventory

T → Open trade panel

Esc → Close shop

Tips

Keep planting and harvesting to grow your lucky block collection.

Use Stardust strategically for rare blocks or faster growth.

Trade with other players to get the seeds you need.

Pay attention to Blue Moons for maximum rewards!
